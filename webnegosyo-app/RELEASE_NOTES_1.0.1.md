# WebNegosyo Admin — v1.0.1 release notes

## "What's New" (App Store Connect — recommended)

```
This update turns the app into a full front-of-counter tool, not just an order monitor.

Register (POS)
• Ring up walk-in sales right from your phone or tablet — product grid with photos, running cart, and quantity badges.
• Cash tender screen with a quick-amount keypad and change calculation.
• Counter sales are saved alongside your online orders, so one day's report covers everything.
• New web orders now pop up on the Register with an alert, so you never miss one while serving a customer in front of you.

Inventory
• Track ingredients and stock levels in the app.
• Dishes are hidden from your online menu automatically when an ingredient runs out — and come back the moment you restock.
• Cancelling an order returns its stock to the shelf.

Menu editing
• Build and edit modifier groups, variations, and add-ons directly in the app — no laptop needed.
• Product photos load faster throughout.

Staff
• Give each staff member access to only the parts of the app they need.

Fixes
• Fixed a crash that could occur right after signing in.
• Fixed duplicated screen reloads when switching tabs.
• General stability and performance improvements.
```

Character count: ~1,180 (limit 4,000).
