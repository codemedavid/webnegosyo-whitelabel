# Release notes — 1.0.4 (build 39)

Ships against live store version 1.0.3. Covers every merchant-facing change
between the 1.0.3 build (`ad17fae5`) and build 39 (`51d242ae`).

---

## "What's New in This Version" — App Store Connect

> Paste this into the *What's New in This Version* field. 2,050 characters,
> well inside Apple's 4,000-character limit.

```
We're now SmartMenu — same app, same login, a new name and look on your home screen.

This is our biggest update yet.

KITCHEN DISPLAY
A live kitchen board shows every ticket as it comes in, so your cooks can work off a screen instead of paper.

PRINT WHERE IT MATTERS
Connect more than one printer and give each a job — receipts at the counter, order chits in the kitchen. Kitchen tickets now print automatically the moment an order is confirmed, even if nobody is looking at the app. Bluetooth printer discovery and printing on iPhone and iPad are also much more reliable.

YOUR RECEIPTS, YOUR WAY
Design your receipt in Receipt Studio: pick a layout, add your logo, choose which details appear, and decide when receipts print. Every receipt can now carry a QR code customers scan to track their order.

DELIVERY FROM THE REGISTER
Take a delivery order at the counter — address, contact number, and a delivery fee you set yourself. Got the fee wrong? Correct it on an order you've already placed. Lalamove booking now works for every store.

SCHEDULED ORDERS
A dedicated agenda for advance orders, with reminders before each one is due, so pre-orders stop slipping through.

YOUR TEAM
Add and manage staff right in the app, control what each person can open, and pin the screen each account starts on.

STOCK THAT KEEPS UP
Build recipes so dishes draw down ingredients automatically, and get a warning at the register before you sell something you can't make.

EXPORT YOUR DATA
Export orders, daily sales, and customers as CSV and share them anywhere.

ALSO IN THIS RELEASE
• Add another round to an order the kitchen is already cooking
• Tell customers when their food will be ready with a prep time you set
• Scan-to-collect pickup, which you can switch on or off
• New order push notifications now reach every store
• Service charges and edited delivery fees are named clearly on totals
• Fixed a freeze when ringing up a second sale back-to-back
• Many stability and speed improvements throughout

Thanks for running your store with us. Questions or ideas? We read every message.
```

## Notes for whoever submits

- The store listing is still titled **WebNegosyo**; the app's home-screen name
  is **SmartMenu**. The first line of the notes exists to absorb that mismatch.
  If the listing name is changed to SmartMenu in App Store Connect before this
  release goes live, drop that opening line.
- Nothing here mentions internal backends, tenants, or infrastructure — Apple
  reviewers and merchants both read this field.
