Hello, and thank you for the detailed feedback.

## Guideline 3.1.1 — Business — removed account registration

We have **removed the account registration feature** for businesses and
organizations from the app. The previous build included a "Create your store"
form; that screen and every entry point to it have been deleted.

The app is now **sign-in only** for existing merchant accounts, plus a read-only
**Demo** mode that requires no account. New merchant stores are provisioned by our
team through an off-app onboarding process — the app itself offers no registration,
purchase, subscription, or paywall of any kind.

Demo access and a real throwaway merchant account (for the Account → Delete Account
flow required under 5.1.1) are provided in App Review Information.

## Guideline 2.3.3 — Accurate Metadata — screenshots

We have uploaded new **6.5" iPhone** and **13" iPad** screenshots that show the
current version of the app in use — the live order dashboard, order queue, and
analytics screens — rather than the login screen. These reflect the app's core
functionality for merchants managing their store.
